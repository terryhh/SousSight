- Run, study, change and share the code under the AGPL.

- Use the marks unchanged when talking about this project, for example in articles, talks or reviews.

- Distributing or hosting a **modified** version under the SousSight name or logo. Rename your fork and change its icon.

To ask for permission, open an issue on this repository.
