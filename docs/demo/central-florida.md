# Why this matters in Central Florida

Draft narrative for the video voice-over and the submission text. Every sentence describes what SousSight is built to do or where the idea came from. None of it claims an outcome. Nothing here has been measured with real households yet, so the narrative includes no counts, savings or adoption figures.

## Short version (about 30 seconds of voice-over)

> Food pantries across Central Florida, including UCF's campus pantry and its garden-to-pantry work, help people take home groceries. Groceries alone don't make you confident at the stove, though. Canned beans, a loaf of bread and a tomato only turn into a meal if you know what to do with them. SousSight is a cooking coach that looks at what you have, talks with you while you cook, and asks you instead of guessing. It needs no account, works in the phone browser you already have, and can speak Spanish.

## Longer version (submission text)

**Inspiration.** UCF's [garden-to-pantry program](https://www.ucf.edu/pegasus/how-does-your-garden-grow/) and [FreshU cooking demos](https://whps.sswb.ucf.edu/cooking-demos-classes/) show that people learn to cook from pantry food when someone shows them how. A live demo reaches the people who attend it. SousSight tries to bring a patient coach to a household's own counter, when they are actually cooking.

**Who it is for.** Students, first-time cooks, and families stretching pantry staples into meals. It suits anyone who would rather point a phone at the counter than search through recipes.

**Design choices aimed at these households:**
- **No account and no install.** It opens in Chrome on Android or a desktop. Anonymous sign-in happens behind the scenes.
- **It starts from what you already have.** You scan, say or type what you got, and the meals are ranked by what is on hand. The missing items are shown, not assumed.
- **Find food help nearby** lists pantries and stores near Orlando, with Google Maps links and grounded results only.
- **Allergy check first.** The coach asks about the recipe's allergens before it gives guidance. It is clear that the camera cannot confirm an allergen is absent.
- **The cook decides.** Substitutions, progress and household texts all need the cook's confirmation. Nothing is sent automatically.
- **Spanish voice.** Gemini Live answers in the language the cook speaks. This helps households who are more comfortable cooking in Spanish.
- **Low cost to run.** Sessions are capped, idle sessions stop, and the delivery-outcome demo runs without sending paid SMS.

## What we are not claiming

- That SousSight has been used by pantry clients or has improved anyone's cooking, nutrition or food budget.
- That it is affiliated with or endorsed by UCF or any pantry. We cite their public programs only as inspiration.
- That vision can judge food safety, internal temperature, freshness or allergens.

When real users try it, record what they did and what happened on a card before adding any outcome to this narrative.
