## Before recording

- [ ] Re-read the evidence cards and update the status table in [README.md](README.md#evidence-status-by-segment). Note the deployed commit from the latest successful deploy run.
- [ ] Device and browser written down: phone model, Android version and Chrome version, or desktop OS and Chrome version.
- [ ] App in real mode (no **Rehearsal** badge), unless the take is deliberately a rehearsal.
- [ ] Recipe shows **Peanut Butter Sandwich**. A **New cook** was started for this take.
- [ ] Camera and mic allowed. Do-not-disturb on. Battery above 50%, or the device plugged in.
- [ ] Ingredients and props from [runbook.md §1](runbook.md#1-ingredients-and-props) are on the counter.
- [ ] Frame checked: no faces of others, screens, addresses or phone numbers.
- [ ] If beats 8–9 are in the take: simulation flag deployed, and the cook has the allowlisted demo recipient from Terry off-camera ([runbook.md §5a](runbook.md#5a-simulated-outcomes-beats-89-no-sms)). `ENABLE_DINNER_MESSAGES` stays `false`.
- [ ] Real SMS: **not planned** unless Terry has given fresh approval of the recipient and text ([runbook.md §5b](runbook.md#5b-real-sms-not-planned)).

## While recording

- [ ] Screen capture runs on the cooking device, or a second camera films the screen. Check that the audio of both the cook and the coach is captured.
- [ ] Each beat is marked live, simulated or rehearsal at the moment it happens, using a note sheet or spoken aloud.
- [ ] Record the timer insert (salsa) as a separate take.
- [ ] Press **Stop** at the end and capture the camera and mic indicators turning off.

## After recording

- [ ] Caption every simulated or rehearsal beat in the edit, and say on the end card which parts were simulated.
- [ ] Remove or blur any recipient number, notification or bystander.
- [ ] Keep raw camera and mic footage out of GitHub. Commit only redacted stills, if any.
- [ ] Record the per-beat results (live, simulated, rehearsal or failed) on [SS-040 #95](https://github.com/terryhh/SousSight/issues/95) and the matching SS-044–SS-048 card.
- [ ] Note any real Gemini or Vonage usage from the takes in [COST-LEDGER.md](../COST-LEDGER.md) (Terry).
- [ ] The backup video goes to [SS-048 #113](https://github.com/terryhh/SousSight/issues/113).
